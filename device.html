2. LIVING CHLOROPHYLL-A EXTRACTION
Simple Protocol (High Fluorescence Yield):
  1. Macerate 20g fresh organic baby spinach or Spirulina powder in a mortar with 50mL of 80% food-grade ethanol or pure vegetable glycerin.
  2. Filter through a 0.2µm PTFE syringe filter to obtain a particle-free, optical-grade solution.
  3. Under standard light, the fluid is emerald green. When the 405nm violet beam passes through, it emits intense ruby-red fluorescence at 668nm.
  4. Pour into the quartz cuvette, seal with Viton O-rings, and tighten the knurled top crown nut.
