# QORA: Visual Direction

## Aesthetic Philosophy

QORA's visual language lives at the intersection of **biological complexity** and **quantum abstraction**. The aesthetic should feel:

- **Organic but precise** — like looking at a protein crystal under a microscope
- **Ethereal but grounded** — quantum phenomena rendered tangible
- **Responsive** — the visual changes based on presence, creating a living artwork

---

## Key Visual Elements

### 1. Protein Scaffold Sculpture (Physical Object)

**Form:** 3D-printed or resin-cast representation of the Fenna-Matthews-Olson (FMO) complex — the trimeric protein found in green sulfur bacteria where quantum coherence in photosynthesis was first observed.

**Scale:** 2-3 meters tall (visceral, human-scale)

**Material:**
- Option A: Frosted resin with internal LED — light passes through the protein channels
- Option B: Polished aluminum — cold, reflective, contrasting the "warm" quantum concept
- Option C: Living biofilm on a scaffold — actual photosynthetic organisms growing on the sculpture surface

**Visual impact:** The sculpture should look like something between a coral reef and a microprocessor — organic structure, computational purpose.

**Reference images to source:**
- FMO complex crystal structure (PDB: 3ENI)
- Dilution refrigerator golden chandelier (Google Sycamore, IBM Quantum)
- Coral reef formations (branching, porous structures)
- Bone trabecula (natural load-bearing architecture)

### 2. Projection Mapping (Zone 2)

**Visual style:** Particle system visualization of exciton transport through the protein matrix.

**Color palette:**
- Deep violet/blue → cyan → emerald (photosynthetic energy gradient)
- White flash points (quantum coherence events)
- Fade to warm amber (decoherence, energy dissipation)

**Behavior:**
- **Empty room:** Smooth, coherent particle flows along protein pathways — the "dreaming" state
- **One visitor:** Slight perturbation — particles ripple near the person
- **Crowded room:** Full decoherence cascade — the visualization fractures, particles scatter, the system "wakes up" and the quantum dream collapses

**Technical approach:**
- WebGL/Shadertoy real-time particle simulation
- Protein scaffold geometry as the particle constraint volume
- Depth camera (Azure Kinect / RealSense) for visitor tracking
- Audio-reactive elements (ambient sound modulates particle density)

### 3. Living Culture Display (Zone 3)

**Setup:** Petri dish or bioreactor containing photosynthetic cyanobacteria (e.g., *Synechocystis* or *Spirulina*).

**Imaging:** Fluorescence camera captures chlorophyll emission (red glow under blue/UV excitation).

**Display:** The fluorescence image feeds back into the Zone 2 projection — the biological quantum system directly drives the artistic visualization.

**Aesthetic:** Raw, scientific, unpolished. The contrast between the polished sculpture (Zone 1) and the wet, alive culture (Zone 3) creates tension.

**Reference:** Anna Dumitriu's bacterial art work, Eduardo Kac's GFP Bunny, Joe Davis's microbial art.
